How it works

ਇਹ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ

There are no ready-made labels saying which verses mean the same. The system learned it from the commentators.

Read in context

A single line is often half a thought. Each Pankati is read with its pada, the lines up to its ॥n॥ marker, so the meaning comes from the whole idea.

Meaning + words

A BGE-M3 model, fine-tuned on padas that two independent Steeks explain alike, is combined with keyword search. Matches with no shared words get their own section.

Explained from the Steeks

Every result carries a one-line reason drawn from the Steeks. "Why this match?" goes further: it compares the two padas using only their Steeks and says plainly whether they are the same, related, or different.

Semantic Retrieval and Contextual Exploration System

A B.Tech major project for Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji. Pankatis that teach the same thing are often written in completely different words, and keyword search cannot connect them. This system learns meaning from three published Steeks (listed at the bottom of the page).
